What is D.I.M?

I have long been frustrated with my tendency to turn to digial technologies such as YouTube, Instagram, and Reddit to temporarily alieviate discomfort or stress in my day, with YouTube being the main offender.

This behaviour may feel good in the moment [and indeed it may not], but it never results in a reduction of stress -- in fact, quite the opposite. Time is lost and stress is gained.

I have rarely successfully combated this phenomemon. D.I.M. is intended as a strict break from these technologies, to prevent the undesirable behaviours.

Methods and Rules

I will be using this webpage, and the iPhone app "Easlo Experiments" to track and record this project.

The rules I am imposing for myself are:

  • No YouTube whatsoever
  • No Instagram, except checking messages through the Beeper app
  • No Reddit, except for very specific and immediately applicable issues [such as any that may occur in my hobby computing projects]. Any such Reddit usage will be logged on this webpage for accoutability purposes.

In order to give me something actionable and positive to replace these technologies with in moments of stress, I will be using an intervention activity. To begin with, this will be a set of pushups, to get blood moving, my mind focused on something, and good chemicals released into my brain while doing something constructive. The intervention is open to change.
