Why make a website?

Hopefully, there will be updates and tinkering galore in the future of this website. Expect changes!!

As hinted at in the welcome, I also have a continuing interest in moving away from the tyrannical clutches of "Big Tech". This indie-web inspired site is one of my ways of manifesting that conviction.
